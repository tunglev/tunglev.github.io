Catafy is a web application that enables users to create their own e-commerce store in under 3 minutes. The platform targets small businesses, startups, young entrepreneurs, content creators, and influencers.

### My Contributions

- Engineered backend integration with Firebase (Authentication, Storage, Databases)
- Participated in both front-end and back-end development
- Designed the application's UI/UX
- Collaborated with team members to manage project workflow
- Prepared and delivered presentations for investors
- Conducted research on visual identification and brand strategy

### Technologies

React, JavaScript, HTML/SCSS, Firebase (Firestore, Authentication, Storage), GitHub

### What I Learned

This was an early project that taught me a lot about full-stack development, team collaboration, and the importance of clean UI/UX design. The experience of pitching to investors also sharpened my communication and presentation skills.
