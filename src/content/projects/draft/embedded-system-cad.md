This is a collection of my work in embedded systems, hardware prototyping, circuit design, and CAD modeling. I enjoy the full pipeline from concept to physical prototype.

### Technical Skills Demonstrated

- **Hardware Design** — Circuit boards, component selection, signal integrity, power management, thermal analysis
- **Embedded Programming** — Real-time systems, interrupt handling, peripheral interfacing
- **CAD & 3D Mechanical Modeling** — Enclosure design, part modeling, assembly, manufacturing considerations
- **System Integration** — Hardware-software co-design, sensor integration, system testing

### Tools & Platforms

- **Microcontrollers:** Arduino, ARM Cortex
- **Communication:** I2C, SPI, UART
- **PCB Design:** KiCad, Altium Designer
- **CAD Modeling:** SolidWorks, Fusion 360
- **Debugging:** Oscilloscopes, logic analyzers

### What I Love About This

There's something special about hardware — when your code compiles, flashes, and a physical device comes to life. The intersection of software and hardware is where some of the most impactful engineering happens.
