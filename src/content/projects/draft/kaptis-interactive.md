Kaptis Interactive is my indie game development studio, with our first game released in 2022. The vision is to form global teams creating games that can be played by many, with an emphasis on developer independence and freedom.

### Studio Vision

I believe great games come from passionate teams who genuinely want to work together. Kaptis is built around the idea of giving developers the freedom to form teams with people they want to work with, rather than being constrained by traditional studio structures.

### Games Released Under Kaptis

- **Flight Physics Simulator** — Realistic flight aerodynamics simulator (Google Play)
- **Blimp: Showdown** — 2-4 player strategy build & battle game (Google Play)
- **Shotgun Birb** — Physics-based local multiplayer battle game (Google Play)
- **AR Experiment Space** — Sandbox of mini AR sport games

### Technologies

Unity, C#, Mobile Development, AR/VR
