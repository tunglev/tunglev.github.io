After completing Professor David Churchill's COMP 4300 course, I built this ECS (Entity-Component-System) game engine to understand what goes on "behind the curtains" of game engines.

### What I Built

This project explores the fundamental architecture of game engines — turning complex mathematical challenges into virtual experiences. The ECS pattern separates data (components) from behavior (systems), making the engine modular and extensible.

### Key Learnings

- Deep understanding of game engine architecture and systems
- Crafting dynamic gameplay mechanics and architecting immersive environments
- Proficiency in C++ as a tool for building systems that bring imagination to life
- Memory management, performance optimization, and data-oriented design
