Simply Metro is a minimalist 2D metro simulator game I created. The focus was on clean, calming aesthetics and satisfying gameplay loops — managing a metro system without the complexity of traditional simulation games.

### Design Philosophy

I wanted to create something minimal but engaging. The game strips away the clutter of typical sim games and focuses on the core joy of watching a metro system you built come to life. Clean lines, simple controls, satisfying feedback.

### Technologies

Unity, C#, 2D rendering

### Platforms

Planned future release on **Windows**.
