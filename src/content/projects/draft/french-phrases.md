In French culture, politeness begins with a warm greeting. Always say 'Bonjour' upon entering a boutique, bakery, or bistro before making any request. It serves as the social handshake that sets a positive tone for your entire interaction.

From navigating the Paris Métro to discovering cozy neighborhood markets in Lyon, mastering key everyday phrases ensures a confident and pleasant travel experience.

### Essential polite etiquette & key phrases:

- **Bonjour / Bonsoir** — Good morning / Good evening
- **S'il vous plaît** — Please (Formal & respectful)
- **L'addition, s'il vous plaît** — The check, please
- **Où sont les toilettes?** — Where is the restroom?
- **Merci beaucoup** — Thank you very much
