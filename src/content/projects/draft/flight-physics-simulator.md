I've always been an aviation geek, so building a realistic flight simulator was a natural passion project. The Flight Physics Simulator is my attempt to create authentic aerodynamics that feel right — not just game-like physics, but real flight dynamics.

### Development Journey

This started as a personal project and has been under consistent updates. I wanted to capture what makes flying feel real — the way lift changes with airspeed, how drag affects your glide path, the subtle effects of crosswinds. It's been a fascinating deep dive into aeronautical engineering concepts.

### Platforms

Available on **Google Play** now, with planned future releases on **iOS** and **Windows**.

### Technologies

Unity, C#, custom physics simulation, mobile optimization

### What I Learned

Building this simulator taught me a ton about applied physics — translating real aerodynamic principles into game engine code that runs smoothly on mobile devices. It's one thing to understand lift and drag on paper, but quite another to implement it in real-time simulation.
