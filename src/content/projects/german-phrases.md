Planning a trip to Germany? While many Germans in major cities speak English fluently, initiating conversations with basic German greetings demonstrates respect and opens doors to much warmer local interactions.

Whether you are ordering coffee at a local Kaffeerösterei in Berlin, buying regional train tickets, or settling the bill at a traditional Bavarian Gasthaus, a handful of high-utility phrases go a very long way.

We compiled this minimalist guide to help you navigate everyday travel scenarios comfortably without feeling overwhelmed by complex grammar rules.

### Top high-frequency German expressions:

- **Guten Tag / Hallo** — Hello & Good day (Universal polite greeting)
- **Danke schön / Vielen Dank** — Thank you very much
- **Die Rechnung, bitte** — The check/bill, please
- **Sprechen Sie Englisch?** — Do you speak English?
- **Entschuldigung** — Excuse me / Pardon
