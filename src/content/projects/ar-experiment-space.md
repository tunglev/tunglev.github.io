AR Experiment Space is my personal sandbox for experimenting with different real-world capabilities of AR Core. It features mini sport games designed to be played at home or in the office.

### Experiments & Demos

- **Office Golf** — Play golf using your actual office environment as the course
- **Shadow Casting** — Experiments with shadow casting on transparent planes to create a convincing ground illusion
- **3D Object Rendering** — Rendered a 3D Earth model in AR space

### Future Plans

I'm exploring Hand/Face Tracking, Dynamic Lighting, and Google's Geospatial Creator for photorealistic 3D tiles. The goal is to solve real-world problems outside of gaming using AR.

### Technologies

AR Core, Unity, C#, Google Geospatial API (planned)
