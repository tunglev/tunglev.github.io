This was my entry for the **NASA Space Apps Challenge 2023** under the challenge "Titan City, 3023." The goal was to create an interactive and engaging introduction to what it might take to terraform Titan (or other similarly hostile environments).

### The Challenge

Titan, Saturn's largest moon, has a thick atmosphere and liquid methane lakes — making it one of the most interesting candidates for terraforming in the solar system. The game walks players through the scientific and engineering challenges of making Titan habitable.

### Recognition

The project was awarded **$10,000 in AWS credits** by a panel of industry-leading experts from **NASA, Harvard, and Silicon Valley startups** after I virtually presented the project.

### Technologies

HTML, CSS, JavaScript, custom game logic

### What I Learned

This project taught me how to take complex scientific concepts and turn them into an engaging interactive experience. Balancing educational accuracy with fun gameplay was a rewarding challenge.
